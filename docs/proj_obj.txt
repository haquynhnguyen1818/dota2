        Objective 2 - Pick phase: to produce a suggestion list of heroes to pick based on the opponents' heroes picks.
            Rules:
            1. Opponents can pick up to 5 heroes
            2. With each pick we should able to suggest 3 lists of top 10 best and top 10 worst carry, midlane and offlane by advantages.
            3. The suggestion lists base on the sum of advantage figures against opponents picks. E.g: 
                if opponent's first pick is meepo then the potential carry hero of Troll Warlord of 1.82% advantage is accounted.
                if then opponent's second pick is anti-mage then the potential carry hero of Troll Warlord of 4.72% advantage is accounted.
                Then the total advantage of the potential carry Troll Warlord is 6.54% = (1.82%+4.72%).
                We do the same for opponents' 3rd, 4th and 5th picks.
            4. Note, the script is iteractive by user inputs. The input is opponent picks. Each pick returns 3 lists. These lists get updated by the next input.
            5. Furthermore, we have to assign weights to advantage figures depending on the opponents picks are supports or non-supports (carry, mid, offlane). Weights are 0.8 for supports and 1.0 for non-supports. Then the total advantage of the potential carry = 0.8*advantages against supports + 1.0*advantages against non-supports.

    STEPS
        1. Pulling duo heroes synergy data: combo 2 heroes with good laning phase win rates and matching winning win rates from stratz.
        under each hero's page there's a synergy tab. STRATZ uses a "TrueSynergy" metric — instead of just raw win rate together, it calculates the expected win rate from each hero's individual performance and shows the offset, so you can tell if a pairing overperforms or underperforms expectations. Good for filtering noise from small-sample combos.
        2. Brainstorm on how to incorporate this into hero picks suggestion.
            Few notes:
                a. Shrink each delta by sample size before using it: This part matters a lot in Dota specifically because hero-pair and hero-vs-hero sample sizes vary wildly (some pairs have 100k+ games, some rare pairs have a few hundred). Use empirical Bayes shrinkage:

                    δ_adjusted = δ_raw × (n / (n + k))

                where n is the match count for that pair and k is a smoothing constant (~300–1000 games is reasonable). Without this, a 3-game sample showing a wild synergy swing will dominate your score inappropriately

                b. Lane relevance: a synergy number is only meaningful if the pairing actually interacts (same lane, or a jungle/rotation-based synergy). Pulling overall synergy for two heroes who never share a lane muddies the signal — where possible, prefer STRATZ's laning-specific or role-specific splits over the blanket "with" number.
